Approach

Three habits that have held up across every estate.

Lineage before dashboards

A report nobody can trace is an opinion with a chart. I start from where the data enters and work forward, so every number downstream has a path back.

Owners, not custodians

Governance fails quietly when everyone is responsible and no one is accountable. I push for a named owner on every critical element, with the authority to say no.

State the edge of the map

I bound what I know precisely and name what I don't. It has cost me nothing in interviews and saved me plenty in delivery.
